## 🗂️ Core Categories & Topics

### 1. 📂 [[First Principles & Mental Models|01. First Principles & Mental Models]]
- [[First Principles Thinking in Software Architecture]] — Deconstructing complex engineering problems to their fundamental physical and mathematical truths.
- [[Mechanical Sympathy & Hardware Reality]] — Writing software that works with hardware architecture: CPU caches, branch predictors, memory buses, SSDs.
- [[Trade-Off Analysis (CAP Theorem, PACELC, Amdahl Law)]] — Systematic evaluation of competing engineering priorities: consistency vs availability, latency vs throughput.
- [[Second-Order Thinking & Unintended Consequences]] — Anticipating long-term feedback loops, behavioral incentives, and cascading systemic failures.
### 2. 📂 [[System Deconstruction & Problem Solving|02. System Deconstruction & Problem Solving]]
- [[Top-Down Decomposition vs Bottom-Up Synthesis]] — Balancing high-level system requirements decomposition with low-level component composition.
- [[Root Cause Analysis (5 Whys, Fishbone, RCA)]] — Methodical post-incident investigation identifying systemic organizational and technical root causes.
- [[Bottleneck Identification & Theory of Constraints]] — Locating the single most restrictive constraint in a distributed pipeline to maximize system throughput.
- [[Hypothesis-Driven Debugging & Fault Isolation]] — Formulating falsifiable hypotheses, designing binary search isolation tests, and verifying root causes.
### 3. 📂 [[Engineering Decision Making|03. Engineering Decision Making]]
- [[Architecture Decision Records (ADR) Engineering Practice]] — Documenting context, decision, consequences, and alternatives for irreversible technical choices.
- [[Boring Technology Principle & Innovation Tokens]] — Choosing mature, predictable technologies for core infrastructure and reserving innovation for competitive differentiators.
- [[Reversible vs Irreversible Decisions (Type 1 vs Type 2)]] — Accelerating velocity by distinguishing reversible two-way doors from irreversible one-way doors.
- [[Technical Debt Management & Strategic Refactoring]] — Quantifying technical debt interest, refactoring opportunistically, and negotiating dedicated payback budgets.
