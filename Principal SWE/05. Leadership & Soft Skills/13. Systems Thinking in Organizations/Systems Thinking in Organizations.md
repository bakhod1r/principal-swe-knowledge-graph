## 🗂️ Core Knowledge Domains

- 📂 [[Systems Thinking - Mental Models, System Archetypes, and Feedback Loops|01. Systems Thinking Principles and Peter Senge Disciplines]] — Understanding systems dynamics: Reinforcing and balancing feedback loops, system delays, local optimizations causing global degradation, and Peter Senge's 5 disciplines.
- 📂 [[The Cynefin Framework - Navigating Simple, Complicated, Complex, and Chaotic Domains|02. The Cynefin Framework for Decision Making]] — Applying Dave Snowden's Cynefin framework: Categorizing technical and organizational problems, probe-sense-respond in complex systems, and avoiding catastrophic domain collapses.
- 📂 `03. Team Topologies and Organizational Stream Alignment` — Applying Matthew Skelton & Manuel Pais Team Topologies: 4 team types, 3 interaction modes (Collaboration, X-as-a-Service, Facilitating), and cognitive load budgets.
- 📂 `04. Sociotechnical Systems and Inverse Conway Maneuver` — Treating technical architecture and human organizational structures as co-evolving systems, restructuring teams to drive clean software boundaries, and minimizing coordination overhead.
- 📂 [[Wardley Mapping - Situational Awareness, Value Chains, and Evolution (genesis to Commodity)|05. Wardley Mapping and Strategic Value Chain Evolution]] — Mapping technical assets against user needs and evolutionary stages (Genesis, Custom-Built, Product, Commodity), identifying strategic moats, and predicting industry shifts.
- 📂 [[Second Order Thinking - Modeling Ripple Effects and Unintended Consequences|06. Second Order Thinking and Unintended Consequences]] — Evaluating long-term architectural and organizational decisions: 'And then what?', preventing perverse incentives, and mitigating unintended operational drag.
- 📂 [[Goodhart's Law and Campbell's Law - Designing Robust Engineering Metrics|07. Goodhart's Law and Metric Incentive Gaming]] — Why metrics cease to be good metrics when they become targets (lines of code, story points, PR velocity), counteracting metric gaming, and designing balanced metric pairs.
- 📂 `08. High Trust Organizational Culture and Westrum Model` — Dr. Ron Westrum's organizational model: Information flow, psychological safety, inquiry over blame, bridging silos, and building a high-performance generative engineering culture.
- 📂 [[Complex Adaptive Systems in Software Engineering - Antifragility and Emergence|09. Complex Adaptive Systems and Emergent Behaviors]] — Navigating unpredictable sociotechnical emergence, designing antifragile engineering processes (Nassim Taleb), small autonomous teams, and distributed decision-making.
- 📂 `10. Sociotechnical Friction Logs and Bottleneck Analysis` — Maintaining structured friction logs for developer workflows, measuring cross-team blocking dependencies, and removing organizational friction points.
