## 1. Definition
**Crisis Leadership - Incident Command, on Call Health, and Stress Mitigation Theoretical Foundations and Principles** represents a mission-critical leadership capability, behavioral invariant, and organizational engineering standard within **Engineering Management & People Leadership**.
Leading teams during major production outages, adopting Incident Commander roles, managing stakeholder panic, on-call rotation fairness, and preventing alert fatigue. Covering Core leadership principles, interpersonal frameworks, and behavioral invariants.

## 4. Gotchas
- **Misaligned Assumptions & Silent Disagreement:** Failing to explicitly clarify expectations and secure commitment leads to silent misalignment, passive resistance, and project delivery failures.
- **Over-Indexing on Process over People:** Introducing rigid bureaucratic processes that stifle developer autonomy and creativity creates high friction, decreases morale, and leads to team attrition.
