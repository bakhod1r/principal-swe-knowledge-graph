## 🗂️ Core Knowledge Domains

- 📂 [[Engineering Management - People, Process, and Business Strategy|01. What Is Engineering Management and EM Archetypes]] — Understanding the transition from IC to manager, management archetypes (People-first, Tech-lead manager, Process-driver), and the manager's operational flywheel.
- 📂 `02. Effective 1 on 1 Coaching and Career Frameworks` — Structuring meaningful 1-on-1 agendas, asking powerful open-ended questions, active listening, long-term career planning, and building trusting relationships.
- 📂 `03. Performance Reviews, Calibrations, and PIPs` — Writing objective performance reviews, conducting cross-team calibration meetings, designing Performance Improvement Plans (PIPs), and fair evaluations.
- 📂 `04. Hiring Pipelines, Bar Raisers, and Technical Interviews` — Designing bias-free structured interview rubrics, training interviewers, the role of Bar Raisers, evaluating technical competence and cultural contribution.
- 📂 `05. Compensation, Equity, and Promotion Committees` — Understanding leveling frameworks (L3 to L7), equity grants (RSUs vs Options), building promotion packets, and defending candidates in promotion committees.
- 📂 `06. Managing Underperformance and Difficult Conversations` — Applying Kim Scott's Radical Candor framework (Care Personally + Challenge Directly), addressing toxic behaviors early, documenting performance issues, and graceful exits.
- 📂 [[Headcount Planning, Capacity Forecasting, and Engineering Budgeting|07. Team Budgeting, Headcount Planning, and Resourcing]] — Modeling team capacity, justifying headcount to executive leadership, vendor tooling cost budgeting, and allocating engineering resources across product initiatives.
- 📂 [[Crisis Leadership - Incident Command, on Call Health, and Stress Mitigation|08. Crisis Leadership and Incident Incident Management]] — Leading teams during major production outages, adopting Incident Commander roles, managing stakeholder panic, on-call rotation fairness, and preventing alert fatigue.
- 📂 [[Stakeholder Management, Cross Functional Alignment, and Managing Up|09. Stakeholder Management and Managing Up]] — Building strong partnerships with Product, Design, Sales, and Support; communicating risks proactively to directors and VPs, and aligning engineering with company OKRs.
- 📂 [[Organizational Change Management, Re Orgs, and M&a Team Integration|10. Organizational Change Management and M&a Integration]] — Leading teams through structural reorganizations, Kotter's 8-step change model, integrating acquired engineering teams, and maintaining team morale during pivots.
- 📂 `11. Engineering Culture, Retention, and Diversity` — Creating a culture of excellence, autonomy, and purpose; driving diversity and inclusion in hiring, recognizing contributions, and reducing voluntary turnover.
- 📂 [[Remote and Hybrid Team Leadership - Asynchronous Culture and Tooling|12. Remote and Hybrid Engineering Team Leadership]] — Managing distributed global engineering teams across time zones, establishing asynchronous communication norms, documentation-first culture, and virtual team bonding.
