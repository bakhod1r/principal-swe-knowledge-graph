## 4. Gotchas
- **Optimizing Local Subsystems at Global Expense:** Improving a single team's local output metric while creating massive downstream queue bottlenecks for QA, Security, or Operations degrades overall company delivery velocity.
- **Failing to Translate Technical Debt into Financial Risk:** Pitching architectural modernizations using abstract engineering jargon to CFOs and CEOs without demonstrating direct revenue, margin, or risk impact leads to rejected budget proposals.
