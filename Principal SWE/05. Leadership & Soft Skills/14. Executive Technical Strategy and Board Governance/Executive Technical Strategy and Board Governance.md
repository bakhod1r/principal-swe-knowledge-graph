## 🗂️ Core Knowledge Domains

- 📂 `01. Total Cost of Ownership TCO and Financial Engineering` — Calculating comprehensive TCO for technical systems: Cloud compute/storage costs, engineering maintenance hours, operational toil overhead, and license fees.
- 📂 `02. Capital Allocation CapEx vs OpEx in Modern Engineering` — Financial accounting principles for engineering leaders: Software capitalization rules (ASC 350-40), optimizing operational cloud spend (FinOps), and maximizing ROI.
- 📂 [[M&A Technical Due Diligence - Evaluating Codebases, Architecture, and Tech Debt|03. Mergers and Acquisitions M a Technical Due Diligence]] — Conducting rigorous technical audits during acquisitions: Code quality, security posture, license compliance (GPL/copyleft), architectural scalability, and team talent assessment.
- 📂 [[Writing Executive 6 Page Narrative Memos and 1 Page Press Releases (pr Faq)|04. Writing Amazon Style Six Page Narrative Memos]] — Amazon's structured narrative decision format: The Press Release / FAQ (Working Backwards), framing executive context, analyzing trade-offs, and driving board-level decisions.
- 📂 [[Boardroom Technical Leadership - Presenting Systemic Risk and Cyber Posture|05. Presenting Technical Risk and Cyber Posture to the Board]] — Communicating technical debt, disaster recovery RTO/RPO, and cybersecurity vulnerabilities to Board Audit Committees without jargon, framing in enterprise financial risk terms.
- 📂 `06. Enterprise Software Vendor and Cloud Contract Negotiation` — Negotiating enterprise SaaS and cloud contracts (AWS EDP, GCP CUD, Datadog): Volume discounting, multi-year tiering, avoiding auto-renew traps, and egress waiver clauses.
- 📂 `07. Technical Debt Balance Sheets and Financial ROI Models` — Modeling technical debt interest payments in developer productivity hours, presenting technical debt refactoring ROI to CFOs, and securing dedicated modernization budgets.
- 📂 [[Scale Up Governance - IPO Readiness, Sox 404, Soc2 Type Ii, and Iso 27001|08. Scale Up Governance, IPO Readiness, and Compliance Audits]] — Scaling governance for public company readiness: Change management audit trails, separation of duties (SoD), access controls, disaster recovery testing, and external audits.
- 📂 [[The Executive Build vs Buy vs Partner Decision Framework|09. Strategic Build vs Buy vs Partner Decision Frameworks]] — Evaluating core business differentiation vs commodity software: Time-to-market advantage, long-term engineering maintenance liability, vendor viability, and exit strategies.
- 📂 [[Multi Year Technology Vision - Mckinsey Three Horizons of Growth Framework|10. Multi Year Technology Vision and Strategic Horizons]] — Horizon 1 (core business optimization), Horizon 2 (emerging platform capabilities), Horizon 3 (disruptive bets like AI/Web3), and balancing R&D portfolio investments.
