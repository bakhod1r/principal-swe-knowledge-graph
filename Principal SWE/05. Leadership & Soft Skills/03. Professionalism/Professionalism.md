## 🗂️ Core Knowledge Domains

- 📂 [[What Is Professionalism - the Software Craftsmanship Ethos|01. What Is Professionalism in Software Engineering]] — Taking full ownership of code quality, refusing to ship unverified defects, continuous career education, and professional accountability.
- 📂 [[The Discipline of Saying No - Defending Quality with Data and Alternatives|02. Saying No with Trade Offs and Alternatives]] — Communicating realistic constraints to product managers, resisting reckless pressure to cut corners, and offering viable scope reduction alternatives.
- 📂 [[Honest Estimation, Precision vs Accuracy, and Firm Commitments|03. Honest Estimation and Deadline Commitments]] — Distinguishing estimates (probability distributions) from commitments (promises), communicating confidence intervals, and eliminating wishful thinking.
- 📂 `04. Continuous Learning and Deliberate Practice` — Dedication to continuous improvement outside work hours, code katas, reading computer science papers, and mastering new paradigms.
- 📂 [[Ethical Software Engineering, User Privacy, and Societal Impact|05. Ethical Engineering and Code Responsibility]] — ACM Code of Ethics, protecting user privacy, refusing to implement deceptive dark patterns or malicious telemetry, and professional integrity.
- 📂 `06. Testing As a Professional Moral Imperative` — Treating automated unit and integration tests as a non-negotiable professional standard, test coverage as a byproduct of good design, and QA partnership.
- 📂 [[Eliminating Hero Culture - Sustainable Pace and 40 Hour Focus|07. Avoiding Hero Culture and Sustainable Pace]] — Why heroics, midnight hackathons, and extreme crunch indicate systemic management failure; maintaining a steady 40-hour sustainable rhythm for marathon excellence.
- 📂 [[Professional Work Ethic, Humility, and Egoless Programming|08. Professional Work Ethic and Humility]] — Separating personal identity from code, accepting constructive critique with grace, continuous curiosity, and respecting teammates' expertise.
- 📂 `09. Clean Code Standards and Craftsmanship Discipline` — Leaving every codebase cleaner than you found it, refactoring continuously during feature development, and writing readable, self-documenting code.
- 📂 `10. Mentoring Apprentices and Passing Knowledge` — The master-journeyman-apprentice model in software, onboarding newcomers with patience, pair programming, and multiplying engineering talent.
- 📂 `11. Full Ownership of Production Systems` — Bridging the divide between development and operations, monitoring your own services in production, debugging incidents firsthand, and pride in system uptime.
