## 🗂️ Core Knowledge Domains

- 📂 [[The Forward Deployed Engineer (fde) Role - Palantir and AI Operating Models|01. The FDE Role and Palantir Operating Model]] — Defining the FDE archetype: High-velocity customer-facing engineering, embedded client deployments, rapid prototype-to-production pipelines, and acting as the bridge between client problems and core engineering.
- 📂 [[Technical Discovery - De Risking and Scoping Ambiguous Enterprise Requirements|02. Technical Discovery and Scoping Messy Requirements]] — Translating ambiguous client business problems into concrete engineering deliverables, identifying technical blockers early, scoping realistic MVPs, and managing customer expectations.
- 📂 `03. Customer Facing Architecture and Enterprise Integration` — Deploying software into complex customer environments: Air-gapped on-premises data centers, custom enterprise ETL pipelines, legacy mainframe integrations, and hybrid cloud connectivity.
- 📂 `04. Secure Deployment Into Locked Down Customer Environments` — Navigating strict customer security postures: Deploying into customer-owned VPCs/subnets, security accreditation audits (SOC2, HIPAA, FedRAMP), and least-privilege IAM policies.
- 📂 `05. Applied Ai, Rag, and LLM Systems in Customer Deployments` — Deploying custom RAG systems on messy customer unstructured data, integrating vector databases (pgvector/Qdrant), optimizing on-prem LLM inference, and evaluation frameworks.
- 📂 [[Field Stakeholder Management - C Suite Alignment and Managing Scope Creep|06. Stakeholder Dynamics and C Suite Technical Communication]] — Communicating technical progress and business value to non-technical client executives (CTO, CIO, VP), managing political friction between client internal IT and leadership, and resisting scope creep.
- 📂 [[Field Incident Management - Live Debugging in Client Environments and SLAs|07. Field Incident Mitigation, SLA Guarantees, and Triage]] — Troubleshooting critical production issues within foreign customer infrastructure under intense pressure, telemetry without exfiltrating sensitive PII data, and SLA accountability.
- 📂 [[Product Feedback Architecture - Channeling Field Insights to Core Engineering|08. Product Feedback Loop Channeling Field Pain to Core]] — Preventing bespoke client-specific code forks, synthesizing common client pain points into generalized core platform feature requests, and partnering with product managers.
- 📂 `09. Enterprise Dynamic Ontologies and Semantic Data Models` — Building dynamic semantic data ontologies: Mapping relational tables and data lakes into unified business objects (e.g., Aircraft, Patient, Transaction), graph relationships, and action layers.
- 📂 [[Client Handover Architecture - Documentation, Ci Cd Handover, and Client Training|10. Prototype to Production Handover and Client Enablement]] — Ensuring long-term project sustainability: Training customer engineering teams, writing operational runbooks, setting up self-healing CI/CD pipelines, and executing smooth project handovers.
