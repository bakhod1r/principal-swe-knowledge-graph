## 🗂️ Core Knowledge Domains

- 📂 [[Staff Plus Engineering Archetypes - Tech Lead, Architect, Solver, Right Hand|01. Staff Plus Engineering Archetypes and Leadership]] — Understanding Will Larson's 4 Staff archetypes: The Tech Lead (team multiplier), The Architect (systems visionary), The Solver (deep troubleshooter), and The Right Hand.
- 📂 [[Leading and Driving Organizational Change Without Positional Authority|02. Leading and Influencing Without Authority]] — Influencing cross-functional teams and senior leaders through technical credibility, trust-building, active listening, coalition building, and persuasion.
- 📂 [[Writing Game Changing Technical RFCs and Architectural Proposals|03. Writing High Impact RFCs and Technical Proposals]] — Structuring comprehensive RFCs that anticipate objections, detail trade-offs, address security/cost implications, and guide multi-team technical alignment.
- 📂 [[Pitching Technical Initiatives - Securing Executive Buy in and Funding|04. Securing Executive Buy in and Budget for Tech Initiatives]] — Framing technical refactoring and platform modernization in terms of business revenue, customer retention, operational cost reduction, and competitive advantage.
- 📂 `05. Building Multi Year Technical Roadmaps 3 5 Years` — Anticipating business scale 10x in advance, identifying architectural bottlenecks before they occur, and mapping incremental evolutionary milestones.
- 📂 `06. Cross Organization Governance and Technical Standards` — Establishing lightweight cross-team architectural standards, setting technology radars, deprecating legacy stacks, and preventing organizational fragmentation.
- 📂 `07. Evaluating High Stakes Build vs Buy Decisions` — Evaluating total cost of ownership (TCO), core competency vs commodity functionality, engineering maintenance burden, vendor lock-in risks, and decision matrices.
- 📂 `08. Translating Technical Strategy Into Business Outcomes` — Bridging the gap between engineering decisions and corporate strategy, gross margins, infrastructure unit economics, and accelerating time-to-market.
- 📂 `09. Conway's Law, Team Topologies, and Sociotechnical Systems` — Aligning system architecture with team organizational boundaries, minimizing cross-team communication overhead, and applying the Inverse Conway Maneuver.
- 📂 [[Executive Communication for Principal Engineers - Presenting to the Board|10. Executive Communication and Board Presentations]] — Presenting technology roadmaps, system outages, and strategic architecture to Board members and C-level executives with clarity, composure, and brevity.
