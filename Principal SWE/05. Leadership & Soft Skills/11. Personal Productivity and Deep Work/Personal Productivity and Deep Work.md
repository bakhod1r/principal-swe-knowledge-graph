## 🗂️ Core Knowledge Domains

- 📂 [[Cal Newport's Deep Work - Cultivating Intense Focus in Engineering|01. Deep Work and Cognitive Flow State Design]] — Designing distraction-free deep work blocks for complex coding and architectural design, entering flow state, and eliminating shallow task busyness.
- 📂 [[Paul Graham's Maker's Schedule, Manager's Schedule - Defending Time|02. Maker vs Manager Schedules and Focus Protection]] — Understanding the catastrophic cost of a single 30-minute meeting interrupting a developer's 4-hour maker block, batching meetings, and focus hours.
- 📂 [[Calendar Time Blocking, the Eisenhower Matrix, and Ruthless Prioritization|03. Calendar Time Blocking and Priority Management]] — Categorizing tasks by Urgent vs Important, time-blocking dedicated coding/design slots on calendar, saying no to non-essential meetings, and daily planning.
- 📂 [[Minimizing Context Switching, Notification Batching, and Slack Hygiene|04. Minimizing Context Switching and Notification Fatigue]] — The 23-minute cognitive penalty of context switching, turning off real-time notifications, batching Slack/email communications, and protecting cognitive energy.
- 📂 `05. Physical and Cognitive Ergonomics for Engineers` — Optimizing chair/desk posture, monitor eye-level heights, split keyboards, lighting, eye-strain prevention (20-20-20 rule), and brain health.
- 📂 `06. Preventing Technical Burnout and Chronic Stress` — Recognizing early burnout symptoms (emotional exhaustion, cynicism, reduced efficacy), setting firm work-life boundaries, taking true disconnect vacations, and balance.
- 📂 `07. Energy Management vs Time Management` — Scheduling high-complexity architectural problem solving during personal biological peak hours, and saving routine administrative tasks for low-energy dips.
- 📂 [[Asynchronous First Communication - Writing Thoughtful Messages|08. Asynchronous First Communication and Written Culture]] — Defaulting to rich, context-packed asynchronous messages over interruptive synchronous meetings, using Loom/docs for async demos, and respecting team time.
