## 🗂️ Core Knowledge Domains

- 📂 [[The Tech Lead Role - Balancing Code, Architecture, and Team Guidance|01. The Tech Lead Role and Core Responsibilities]] — Defining the Tech Lead role, balancing 30% coding with 70% technical coordination, setting technical vision, and distinguishing Tech Lead from Engineering Manager.
- 📂 [[Technical Project Delivery - Scoping, Milestones, and Sprint Execution|02. Technical Project Delivery and Sprint Planning]] — Breaking down ambiguous epics into deliverable milestones, running sprint planning sessions, unblocking team members, and ensuring predictable delivery velocity.
- 📂 [[Task Breakdown, Accurate Estimation, and Buffer Modeling|03. Task Breakdown, Estimation, and Buffer Modeling]] — Decomposing complex features into sub-day tasks, applying Planning Poker and PERT three-point estimation, modeling uncertainty buffers, and managing stakeholder expectations.
- 📂 [[Delegating Technical Ownership - Architecture vs Implementation|04. Delegating Architecture and Fostering Team Autonomy]] — Avoiding micromanagement while maintaining code standards, assigning feature leads, creating guardrails and paved roads, and empowering team decision-making.
- 📂 [[Code Ownership Models - Collective Ownership vs Strong Service Ownership|05. Code Ownership, Paved Roads, and Team Standards]] — Establishing team coding standards, automating linting rules in CI, defining code ownership boundaries with CODEOWNERS, and building internal paved roads.
- 📂 `06. Developer Onboarding Framework 30 60 90 Days` — Designing structured onboarding journeys, assigning dedicated onboarding buddies, getting the first PR merged on Day 1, and reducing time-to-productivity.
- 📂 `07. Effective Team Rituals Standups, Retros, and Demos` — Eliminating status-update waste in standups, facilitating psychological safety in retrospectives, tracking actionable improvements, and celebrating team wins.
- 📂 [[Managing Technical Debt vs Feature Delivery - the 20% Allocation Rule|08. Managing Technical Debt vs Feature Velocity]] — Quantifying technical debt interest to product stakeholders, securing 20% sprint capacity for refactoring, creating debt registries, and preventing architectural rot.
- 📂 [[Team Performance Metrics - DORA Metrics, Cycle Time, and Throughput|09. Team Velocity, DORA Metrics, and Continuous Delivery]] — Measuring Deployment Frequency, Lead Time for Changes, Change Failure Rate, and Time to Restore Service (MTTR), without weaponizing productivity metrics.
- 📂 `10. Mentoring and Coaching Junior and Mid Level Engineers` — Conducting regular 1-on-1 coaching sessions, deliberate pair programming, active listening, providing actionable growth feedback, and developing talent.
- 📂 `11. Psychological Safety and High Performing Team Dynamics` — Fostering an environment where engineers can ask questions, admit mistakes, challenge decisions without fear, and drive innovation.
- 📂 `12. Blameless Culture and Root Cause Analysis` — Focusing on systemic process and tooling failures rather than human blame, running blameless postmortems, and turning outages into institutional learning.
