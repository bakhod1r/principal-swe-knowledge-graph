## 🗂️ Core Knowledge Domains

- 📂 [[Crucial Conversations in Engineering - Navigating High Stakes Dialogue|01. Crucial Conversations and High Stakes Dialogue]] — Mastering high-stakes discussions where opinions vary, stakes are high, and emotions run strong; creating psychological safety, and finding shared purpose.
- 📂 [[Principled Negotiation - Harvard Negotiation Project and BATNA|02. Principled Negotiation and the BATNA Framework]] — Focusing on underlying interests rather than rigid positions, generating win-win options, establishing objective criteria, and identifying your Best Alternative (BATNA).
- 📂 [[De Escalating Cross Functional Friction Between Product and Engineering|03. De Escalating Cross Functional Engineering Conflicts]] — Resolving tensions between product deadlines and code quality, finding common ground, mediating disputes objectively, and turning conflict into collaboration.
- 📂 `04. Navigating Organizational Politics and Turf Wars` — Understanding informal power dynamics, avoiding destructive political games, building transparent coalitions, and advocating for team initiatives ethically.
- 📂 `05. Building High Trust Coalitions Across Silos` — Breaking down engineering silos, establishing collaborative relationships with Security, Operations, Product, and Sales; and driving company-wide initiatives.
- 📂 `06. Managing Up and Executive Stakeholder Alignment` — Understanding your manager's priorities and pressures, communicating proactive solutions rather than problems, and delivering predictable, high-visibility results.
- 📂 [[Handling Disagreements in Architectural Review Boards - Disagree and Commit|07. Resolving Disagreements in Architectural Review Boards]] — Facilitating constructive architectural debate, evaluating technical trade-offs objectively, avoiding decision paralysis, and uniting behind 'Disagree and Commit'.
- 📂 [[Repairing Damaged Team Trust and Restoring Psychological Safety|08. Rebuilding Broken Team Trust and Psychological Safety]] — Diagnosing root causes of interpersonal friction, taking ownership of leadership mistakes, engaging in transparent open dialogue, and rebuilding team trust.
