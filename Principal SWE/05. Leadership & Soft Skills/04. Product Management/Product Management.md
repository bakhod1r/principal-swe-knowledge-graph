## 🗂️ Core Knowledge Domains

- 📂 `01. Product Strategy, Vision, and Engineering Alignment` — Aligning technical architecture with product vision, understanding market dynamics, and ensuring technology acts as a business enabler.
- 📂 [[Writing High Impact Product Requirements Documents (prds)|02. Product Requirements Documents PRDs and Spec Writing]] — Structuring clear PRDs (Problem, Goals, Non-Goals, User Stories, Acceptance Criteria, Success Metrics), edge case discovery, and engineering review.
- 📂 [[Feature Prioritization Frameworks - Rice, Wsjf, and Kano Model|03. Prioritization Frameworks Rice, Wsjf, and Kano]] — Evaluating Reach, Impact, Confidence, and Effort (RICE), Weighted Shortest Job First (WSJF), Kano customer satisfaction curves, and data-driven roadmapping.
- 📂 `04. Okrs, Kpis, and Outcome vs Output Metrics` — Setting inspiring Objectives and measurable Key Results, avoiding vanity output metrics (lines of code, story points), and focusing on customer business outcomes.
- 📂 [[Continuous Product Discovery and Opportunity Solution Trees|05. Opportunity Solution Trees and Teresa Torres Discovery]] — Mapping business goals to customer opportunities, ideating solutions, testing underlying assumptions with rapid experiments, and derisking products.
- 📂 [[User Research, Customer Empathy, and Usability Testing|06. User Research, Customer Interviews, and Usability]] — Conducting structured customer interviews, observing user behavior, extracting pain points, and synthesizing qualitative feedback into technical requirements.
- 📂 `07. Feature Flags, Dark Launches, and Canary Releases` — Decoupling code deployment from feature release using LaunchDarkly/Unleash, targeted user cohort testing, ring deployments, and instant kill switches.
- 📂 `08. Go to Market Gtm Strategy and Cross Functional Sync` — Coordinating product launches across Marketing, Sales, Customer Success, Legal, and Support; writing internal release notes, and launch day checklists.
- 📂 [[Product Analytics - Event Taxonomy, Funnel Conversion, and Retention|09. Product Analytics, Funnels, and Event Tracking]] — Designing consistent analytics event schemas (Amplitude, Mixpanel, PostHog), tracking user activation funnels, cohort retention curves, and A/B test analysis.
- 📂 `10. Managing Technical Debt with Product Stakeholders` — Translating architectural refactoring into business risk, customer latency impact, and feature velocity gains to achieve shared debt repayment consensus.
- 📂 [[Defining True Minimum Viable Products (mvp) and Rapid Prototyping|11. Minimum Viable Product Mvp Definition and Prototyping]] — Building the smallest possible solution that tests the core value hypothesis, avoiding over-engineering in early discovery, and iterating based on usage data.
- 📂 `12. Stakeholder Roadmap Alignment and Expectation Management` — Moving away from fixed-date waterfall Gantt charts toward outcome-based now/next/later roadmaps, managing executive expectations, and handling scope change.
- 📂 [[Marty Cagan's 4 Big Product Risks - Value, Usability, Feasibility, Viability|13. Product Risk Management (value, Usability, Feasibility, Viability)]] — Assessing whether users will buy/use it (Value), figure out how to use it (Usability), whether we can build it (Feasibility), and if it works for the business (Viability).
- 📂 `14. Competitive Landscape and Technical Moat Analysis` — Analyzing competitor feature sets, identifying proprietary technology advantages, network effects, API ecosystem lock-in, and defensible architectural moats.
