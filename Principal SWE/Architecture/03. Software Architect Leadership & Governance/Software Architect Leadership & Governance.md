## 🗂️ Core Knowledge Domains

- 📂 [[Software Architect Role, Responsibilities, and Technical Vision|01. Architect Responsibilities and Technical Vision]] — Balancing technical depth with breadth, systems thinking, steering technical strategy, and architectural trade-off analysis.
- 📂 [[Architecture Governance and Architecture Review Boards (arb)|02. Architecture Governance and Review Boards]] — Establishing lightweight architectural guardrails, peer review gates, paved paths, and decentralized decision-making.
- 📂 [[Evolutionary Architecture and Automated Fitness Functions|03. Evolutionary Architecture and Fitness Functions]] — Guiding incremental, non-destructive architectural change with automated CI/CD architectural tests (ArchUnit).
- 📂 [[Technical Debt Identification, Quantification, and Modernization|04. Technical Debt Management and Modernization]] — Debt quadrants (Deliberate vs Inadvertent), legacy system modernization strategies, and tech debt budget allocation.
- 📂 [[Architecture Decision Records (adrs) and RFC Workflows|05. ADRs and RFC Decision Frameworks]] — Documenting context, decision drivers, considered options, consequences, and status in immutable version-controlled ADRs.
- 📂 [[Enterprise Application Integration (eai) and Legacy Modernization|06. Enterprise Software Integration]] — Integrating ERP/CRM systems, API gateways, Strangler Fig migrations, and data synchronization patterns.
- 📂 [[Executive Stakeholder Communication and Business Strategy|07. Executive Stakeholder Alignment]] — Translating complex architectural decisions into business ROI, risk mitigation, and executive executive summaries.
- 📂 [[Architectural Trade Off Analysis and Risk Modeling|08. Trade Off Analysis and Risk Modeling]] — ATAM (Architecture Tradeoff Analysis Method), evaluating quality attributes (Scalability vs Latency vs Cost vs Simplicity).
- 📂 [[Developer Enablement, Paved Roads, and Platform Engineering|09. Developer Enablement and Paved Roads]] — Creating reusable architectural templates, scaffolding CLI tools, and internal developer platforms (IDPs).
