# 🏛️ Software Architecture, Enterprise Design & System Paradigms

Comprehensive, production-grade master architecture covering the complete spectrum of software design patterns, architectural styles, and enterprise frameworks across 9 focused master pillars:
- **Classical & Modern Styles:** Monoliths, Modular Monoliths, SOA, Microservices, Event-Driven (EDA), Serverless, Actor Model, Space-Based, P2P, Pipes & Filters, Blackboard.
- **Inversion-of-Control & Boundaries:** Layered N-Tier, Hexagonal Architecture (Ports & Adapters), Onion Architecture, Clean Architecture (Concentric Circles), Screaming Architecture, Dependency Rule, Component Cohesion (REP, CCP, CRP), Component Coupling (ADP, SDP, SAP), Plugin Architecture, and Humble Object Pattern.
- **UI & Presentation Patterns:** MVC, MVP, MVVM, MVI, VIPER, Redux / Elm Architecture, Flux, and Micro-Frontends.
- **Data & Event-Driven Patterns:** CQRS, Event Sourcing, Saga distributed transactions, Outbox Pattern, Change Data Capture (CDC), and Lambda/Kappa architectures.
- **AI & Autonomous Agent Architectures:** Model Context Protocol (MCP), Agent-Tool Loops, Multi-Agent Swarms, Enterprise RAG, and Semantic Memory Graphs.
- **Domain-Driven Design (DDD):** Ubiquitous Language, Aggregates, Repositories, Domain Events, Bounded Contexts, and Context Maps.
- **API Design & Integration Architecture:** REST, GraphQL Federation, gRPC Microservice Mesh, OAuth2/OIDC, OpenAPI Contracts, Rate Limiting & Throttling, and WebSockets/SSE.
- **Software Architect Leadership & Governance:** Software Architect Role, ARB Governance Boards, Evolutionary Architecture Fitness Functions, Tech Debt Modernization, and ADRs.
- **Architectural Anti-Patterns & Pitfalls:** System Shape Anti-Patterns, Boundary & Coupling Anti-Patterns, and Process & Hype Anti-Patterns.
